About Jayabuddha Agrotech

A legacy of agricultural excellence rooted in sustainability, organic farming, and community empowerment.

30+
Years of Excellence

Serving Nepal's Agriculture with Passion & Integrity

Jayabuddha Agrotech is a premier agriculture-based company located in the heart of Bharatpur, Chitwan. For over three decades, we have been at the forefront of producing premium organic honey and beekeeping products while championing sustainable farming practices across Nepal.

From our own farm and nursery, we cultivate high-quality lemon products and provide comprehensive agricultural support services including soil testing, agriculture planning, and hands-on training for farmers and students conducting agricultural research.

Leadership with Vision

Our CEO is a former Joint Secretary of the Ministry of Agriculture, Government of Nepal, and former Secretary of the Agriculture Ministry of Gandaki Province — a lifelong advocate for farmers' rights and sustainable agricultural development in Nepal.

What We Offer

Agricultural Services

Empowering farmers, researchers, and students with scientific expertise, soil analysis, and practical training.

Agricultural Training

Comprehensive training programs for farmers and students covering modern apiculture, organic farming techniques, sustainable agriculture practices, and hands-on field research support.

Soil Testing & Analysis

Scientific soil analysis services to help farmers determine soil composition, NPK nutrient levels, and pH balance — enabling optimized crop yield and sustainable soil conservation.

Agriculture Planning

Strategic farm design and land management plans tailored to local climate conditions, crop rotation cycles, and long-term economic sustainability for farms of all scales.

Everything you need to know about our organic honey products, soil testing, and agricultural services in Chitwan, Nepal.

Our honey is harvested directly from raw, unpasteurized hives in the pristine forests and organic farms of Chitwan Valley, Nepal. It retains all natural active enzymes, pollen, and propolis without added sugars, preservatives, or artificial heat treatment.

Jayabuddha Agrotech is headquartered at Bharatpur-7, Buddhachowk, Chitwan, Nepal. You can visit our plant nursery and farm in person, or place direct instant orders online via WhatsApp (+977 9851159148) or our website contact form.

We provide scientific soil composition analysis (NPK nutrient levels & pH balance), farm planning, beekeeping consultation, and hands-on agricultural research training for farmers, researchers, and agriculture students in Nepal.

Propolis is a potent natural antimicrobial immune shield; Royal Jelly is rich in 10-HDA for cellular vitality and cognitive health; and Bee Pollen provides over 250 active bioavailable nutrients and proteins for daily stamina.
